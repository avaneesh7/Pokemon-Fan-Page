Pokémon is a massive franchise with a universe of collectible characters whose cards feature incredible artwork that fans love to show off. Beyond the pure fun of ripping booster packs and chasing rare top hits, the market for buying, selling, and trading these cards has evolved into a global marketplace where collectors track market trends, evaluate card condition, and make strategic long-term investments across expansions.

About Pokemon

Pokémon is the world's highest-grossing media franchise, built on decades of generational fandom and cultural impact. Its massive global appeal across games, television, and merchandise provides a permanent foundation for everything connected to the brand.

Trading Card Game

The Trading Card Game translates that brand loyalty into tangible, physical collectibles. Driven by iconic character appeal and low pull-rate art variants, collectors treat specific cards as major personal display pieces and prized collection staples.

Community

A dedicated global network of collectors and active marketplaces fuels a high-volume secondary market. Strong daily demand makes buying, trading, and selling cards straightforward, giving the hobby constant liquidity across all price points.

Fun Facts

  • Highest-Grossing Media Empire: Pokémon stands as the highest-grossing media franchise of all time, having generated over $100 billion in global revenue through its mix of video games, trading cards, animated series, and merchandise.
  • Record-Breaking Valuations: The market's ultimate holy grail—the PSA 10 Pikachu Illustrator owned by Logan Paul—sold at auction for a record-breaking $16.5 million, making it the most expensive trading card in history.
  • Sealed Pokémon vs. S&P 500: Out-of-print sealed booster boxes have historically outpaced the S&P 500, with select vintage and modern sets delivering 15%+ annualized returns as physical supply vanishes.
